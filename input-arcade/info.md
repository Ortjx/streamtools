# Input Arcade

A Bongo-Cat-style input cam for your stream. A character leans over a desk with a real keyboard drawn in perspective: its paw lands on the exact key you press and the key lights up, while the other paw holds the mouse, which slides with your cursor and lights its buttons when you click. Pick one of eight characters — cat, black cat, orange cat, bunny, shiba, frog, bear, robot — and recolour any of them, or make it completely yours: every part (body, each paw up and down, keyboard, mouse, desk, background) can be your own picture, mixed freely with ours. Share a skin with a friend as one file. New in 4.5–4.6: game characters with painted hands whose arms come out of their own sleeves (CS2 SAS and the CS Terrorist ship built in), and a desk setup to pick — six keyboards (including one where every key is a rubber duck), six mice, six desks and six mouse pads.

Input Arcade runs completely on your computer. It reads key and button state natively, works offline, needs no account, never reconstructs words, and does not save or send input history.

## Version

4.6.1

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11
- OBS is optional

## Setup

1. Run `Input Arcade.exe` — or switch it on in Workbench › Stream & Tools.
2. Customise it at `http://localhost:7890`.
3. Add `http://localhost:7890/overlay` to OBS as a Browser Source at 600 × 420.
4. Leave it running while you stream.

Your own parts are see-through PNG or GIF pictures the size of the cam (600 × 420), so they line up by themselves. "Both paws on the keyboard" mode is in the customiser.

Settings and skins are stored locally beside the executable (in Workbench, in Workbench's own data folder).
